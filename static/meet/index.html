Meet

Meet Details

Printed on the ranking's cover page, the start cards and the race bibs.

Start Cards

One A4 page per starter with name, starter ID, club, country and every start with its day, relay, time, range and lane.

Race Bibs

One A4 landscape bib per starter with the starter ID, name, club, country, and the meet's name, venue and date.

Lane Assignments (CSV)

Every lane assignment of the meet as a CSV file, one row per start, in time, range and lane order: date, relay, start and end time, range, lane, start ID, starter ID, name, club, country and discipline. Use it e.g. for a mail merge that prints the labels identifying the targets.

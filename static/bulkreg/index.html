Bulk Registration (Demo)

Demo. Registers many starters and their starts in one go, e.g. a whole club. It reads this meet's individual rankings and the registered competitors, but nothing is saved.

New rows take the club and country from here, so a club only types names and rankings.

Paste from Excel

One starter per line, columns separated by tabs (as copied from Excel) or semicolons: Name; Gender; Year of birth; Club; Country; Rankings. Rankings are short names separated by spaces, e.g. WHITO COLT.

Starters

Short names of the rankings

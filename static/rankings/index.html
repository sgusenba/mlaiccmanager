Rankings

What this meet ranks. A ranking has its own name and ranks one or more entries together, each competitor (or team) once, on their best result first. An entry is one discipline, or the sum of several (e.g. Remington_O = Colt + Donald Malson_O). The disciplines the rankings cover are the meet's program: only they take starts and teams. The disciplines themselves are kept on the Disciplines page.

Also limits the team disciplines a team ranking can rank.

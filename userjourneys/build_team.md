Take a configured meet with a team ranking and starts in the disciplines that count for it.
a club enters a team: the organiser picks three starts of the club for the team discipline, leaves the name blank
the team is named after the club, its score is the sum of the members' results
the team shows up in the team ranking and on each member's start card

Variation 1:
one member has no result yet: counts 0, the team is flagged not complete

Variation 2:
a member is already in another team of the same team discipline, or is picked twice: refused

Variation 3:
the same start is used for a second team discipline that counts the same discipline (e.g. Gustav Adolph and GP of Versailles): allowed

Variation 4:
a start of a discipline that does not count for the team discipline is picked: refused

Variation 5:
a member is replaced by another starter after results are in: the team's score follows

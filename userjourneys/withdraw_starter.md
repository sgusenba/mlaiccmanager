Take a fully configured meet with lanes, teams and some results.
a starter with several starts, lanes, a result and a team membership cannot come
the organiser deletes the competitor
the starts, results, lanes and team memberships are gone, the lanes are free again
the team stays, with one member less, and is not complete
the rankings no longer list the starter, the ids are never handed out again

Variation 1:
the starter only drops one discipline: only that start, its lane, its result and its team memberships go, the rest stays

Variation 2:
someone else changed the competitor in the meantime: the delete answers 409 and nothing is deleted

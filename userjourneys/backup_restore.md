Take a fully configured meet with competitors, starts, lanes, teams, results and rankings.
the organiser downloads a backup
then data changes: a competitor is deleted, results are entered, a ranking is changed
the organiser restores the backup
the system is exactly in the state of the backup, a safety copy of the state before the restore exists

Variation 1:
the upload is not a zip, or a zip without data.json: refused, nothing changes

Variation 2:
the safety copy is restored: the state from before the restore is back

Variation 3:
the backup is restored on a fresh installation: the meet is complete there

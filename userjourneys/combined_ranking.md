Take a meet with a ranking that sums disciplines (e.g. Remington_O = Colt + Donald Malson_O)
and a ranking that ranks disciplines together (e.g. Whitworth O/R, set up by the organiser, the default rankings have none).
a starter shoots both disciplines of the sum: ranked once with the two results added up
a starter shoots original and reproduction of the together ranking: ranked once on the better result, the other listed below

Variation 1:
a starter has only one of the two results of the sum: ranked with that one, the missing one counts as nothing

Variation 2:
a starter has two starts in one discipline of the sum: the better one counts

Variation 3:
one of the results is deleted afterwards: the sum and the rank follow

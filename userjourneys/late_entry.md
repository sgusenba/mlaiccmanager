Take a fully configured meet, lanes are assigned and the first day is locked and already shot.
a new starter shows up on the second day and wants to shoot a discipline of that day
the organiser registers the competitor and the start, gives it a free lane in a relay of the day
the result is entered and the starter appears in the ranking and on the start card

Variation 1:
the only free lane is in a relay in which the starter already has a lane on another range: refused, a competitor has one lane per relay

Variation 2:
the organiser tries to put the start on the locked day: refused, the locked day is unchanged

Variation 3:
the discipline fires on another range than the chosen lane: refused

Take a fresh installation with only the shipped discipline catalog, no meet data.
the organiser enters the meet details (name, venue, host, dates)
the organiser loads the default rankings, so the meet has a program
the organiser adds the meet days and relays
afterwards starts can be taken for the disciplines of the program

Variation 1:
before any ranking exists, a start is taken: it must be refused, no discipline is in the program

Variation 2:
the organiser removes a ranking whose discipline already has starts: it must be refused, the starts stay ranked

Variation 3:
the meet's first day is after its last day: refused, the saved details stay

Take a meet with results.
two starters of one ranking have the same score and the same number of 10s, 9s, ... 1s, no tie-break value
both show up on the Todos page
the official measures the targets and enters a tie-break value for each
the Todos page is empty, the lower tie-break value is ranked first

Variation 1:
only one of the two gets a tie-break value: it is ranked first, both stay on the Todos page until the other one has a value too

Variation 2:
both get the same tie-break value: they stay on the Todos page and share the rank

Variation 3:
two teams have the same sum and the same ring counts: the team's tie-break decides

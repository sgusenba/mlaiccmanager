Take a configured meet with competitors and starts, two meet days with relays, no lanes yet.
the organiser unlocks the settings, sets the ranges, relay duration and break, says which rankings are shot on which day
the organiser switches the automatic lane assignment on, it fills the unlocked days
every start has at most one lane, no competitor has two lanes in one relay,
every automatically placed start is on a day whose rankings cover its discipline and on the range its discipline fires on
the starter overview reports no violation, starts without a lane are listed as waiting
the organiser locks the first day

Variation 1:
there are more starts than lanes: the rest is reported as waiting, nothing is put on a wrong day

Variation 2:
the organiser moves a start by hand to a lane another organiser just filled: 409, the lane keeps its start

Variation 3:
after locking, the automatic assignment runs again: the locked day is unchanged

Variation 4:
a discipline is mapped to another range after its lanes were assigned: the overview reports the violation

Variation 5:
a relay with lanes is deleted: its lanes are gone, the later relays move up, the starts wait for a lane again
